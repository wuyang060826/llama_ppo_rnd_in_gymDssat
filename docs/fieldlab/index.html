How to read this comparison

Cards show arithmetic means. Differences use B minus A. Repeat numbers are archival labels and do not establish paired weather seeds. The selected checkpoints illustrate a trade-off in this batch. Baseline comparisons, confidence intervals, and field validation require additional evidence.

EXPERIMENT DESIGN

From observation to action

  1. Observe

    Gym-DSSAT simulates crop growth and supplies numerical field observations.

  2. Encode

    LLaMA encodes observations serialized as text for the PPO actor–critic.

  3. Act

    The policy selects irrigation and nitrogen levels. RND adds an exploration reward during training.
